Welcome to Minelethu Baby Emporium

Minelethu Baby Emporium is a South African baby retail brand under MGM Holdings. We aim to make newborn preparation simple by bringing important baby essentials together in one convenient place.

Our products focus on baby wear, feeding and nutrition-support items, and sanitary and hygiene essentials.
