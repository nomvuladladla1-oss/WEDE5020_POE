About Minelethu Baby Emporium

Minelethu Baby Emporium is a South African baby retail brand MGM Holdings. We provide practical newborn essentials for new and expecting parents

Our products include essential baby wear, feeding and nutrition-support products, and sanitary and hygiene essentials.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To make newborn preparation simple by providing essential baby wear, feeding-support products and hygiene items in one trusted place.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and convenient baby essentials brand for new and expecting parents in South Africa.

Who We Serve

New Parents

Parents preparing for the everyday needs of their newborn baby.

Expecting Parents

Parents preparing their baby essentials before their little one arrives.

Family & Gift Buyers

Family members, guardians and customers looking for practical newborn gifts.
